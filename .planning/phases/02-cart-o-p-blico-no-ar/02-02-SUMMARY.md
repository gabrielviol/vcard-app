- **Files modified:** `apps/web/app/[slug]/qr/route.ts`
- **Verification:** `npx tsc --noEmit` passes; `lib/qr-route.test.ts`'s PNG signature/byte-content assertions still pass; live `next dev` smoke test confirmed a valid 1024x1024 PNG downloads correctly
